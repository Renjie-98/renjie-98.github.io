About个人简介

I am a Research Fellow (Borsa di Ricerca) in the Department of Physics and Astronomy “Galileo Galilei” at the University of Padua, working with Professor Manlio De Domenico and the CoMuNe Lab. Drawing on statistical physics and systems engineering, I study how human-made and natural systems respond to perturbations.

我目前是帕多瓦大学伽利略·伽利莱物理与天文学系的研究员（Borsa di Ricerca），在 Manlio De Domenico 教授及 CoMuNe 实验室开展研究。我的研究结合统计物理与系统工程，探讨人造系统和自然系统如何响应扰动。
